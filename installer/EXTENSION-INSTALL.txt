odm browser extension
=====================

The installer put the extension on your computer, but Chrome, Edge, Brave and
Firefox only let you turn on an extension that is not in their store by hand.
It takes about a minute.

Before you start: make sure the odm download service (odm-daemon) is running.

Where the extension is
----------------------
  Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\odm\extension
  macOS:   ~/Applications/odm/extension
  Linux:   /usr/share/odm/extension

Each of those folders holds two folders: "chrome" and "firefox".

Chrome, Edge, Brave (and other Chromium browsers)
-------------------------------------------------
1. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions, Brave: brave://extensions).
2. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the "chrome" folder.

Firefox
-------
1. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox
2. Click "Load Temporary Add-on..." and choose the file "manifest.json"
   inside the "firefox" folder.
   Firefox removes a temporary add-on when it closes, so repeat this after a
   restart. (Firefox only keeps add-ons that Mozilla has signed.)

Connect it to odm
-----------------
1. Click the odm icon in the browser toolbar, then open Options.
2. Paste the token from the odm token file:
     Windows: %APPDATA%\odm\config\token
     macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/odm/token
     Linux:   ~/.config/odm/token
3. Click "Test Connection".
